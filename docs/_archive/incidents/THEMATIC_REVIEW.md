## Summary of Thematic Alignment

### ✅ **Well-Themed Categories:**
1. **Bosses Defeated** - Boss Kill Shot + combat items only
2. **Score** - General performance items only
3. **Distance** - Survival/travel items only
4. **Coins** - Coin-related items (Force Field, Coin Tractor Beam) + general utility
5. **Enemies Defeated** - Combat/enemy items (Destroy All, Slow Time) + combat items
6. **Streak** - Coin-related items only

### ⚠️ **Categories Needing Review:**
1. **Games Played** - Has Coin Tractor Beam (is this appropriate for general progression?)
2. **Leaderboard** - Has Coin Tractor Beam (is this appropriate for competitive achievements?)

## Questions for Consideration:

1. **Games Played:** Should Coin Tractor Beam be included in general progression rewards, or should it be exclusive to coin-related milestones?
2. **Leaderboard:** Should Coin Tractor Beam be included in competitive rewards, or should it be exclusive to coin-related milestones?
3. **General Items:** Are there any other items that appear in categories where they don't belong thematically?

## Recommended Thematic Rules:

### Coin-Related Items (Force Field, Coin Tractor Beam):
- ✅ **Should appear in:** Coins Collected, Coin Streak
- ⚠️ **Questionable in:** Games Played, Leaderboard
- ❌ **Should NOT appear in:** Bosses Defeated, Score, Distance, Enemies Defeated

### Boss-Related Items (Boss Kill Shot):
- ✅ **Should appear in:** Bosses Defeated, Games Played (elite tier)
- ❌ **Should NOT appear in:** Coins, Score, Distance, Enemies Defeated, Streak

### Enemy-Related Items (Destroy All, Slow Time):
- ✅ **Should appear in:** Enemies Defeated, Score (max tier), Distance (max tier)
- ❌ **Should NOT appear in:** Coins, Bosses Defeated, Streak

### General Items (Orb Level, Extra Lives, Force Field):
- ✅ **Can appear in:** Any category (general utility)
- ⚠️ **Note:** Force Field has dual nature (coin-related via coin streak, but also general protection)
