## Economic Analysis

### Free Item Flow vs. Paid Items

**Free Items Per Month (Active Player):**
- Daily login: ~35-40 items (~$30-$35 value)
- Tournament wins: 0-10 items (if they win, ~$0-$22 value)
- Milestones: 5-15 items (varies by progress)
- **Total: ~40-65 items per month (~$35-$60 value)**

**Paid Item Purchases:**
- If items are $0.50-$3.75 each
- Players might buy 0-5 items per month
- **Revenue per player: $0-$15 per month**

### Key Concerns

#### 1. **Will Players Use Items?**

**Current Situation:**
- Players get 8-9 items per week for free
- Items are consumable (one-time use per game)
- Players might hoard items instead of using them

**Psychology:**
- ✅ **Free items = More likely to use** (no sunk cost)
- ❌ **Expensive items = Less likely to use** (fear of wasting money)
- ⚠️ **Too many free items = Less incentive to buy**

#### 2. **Are Prices Too High?**

**Analysis:**

**Level 1 Items ($0.50-$1.50):**
- ✅ **Reasonable** - Similar to mobile game consumables
- ✅ **Impulse buy range** - Under $2 is impulse purchase territory
- ⚠️ **But** - If players get 8-9 free per week, why buy?

**Special Items ($2.50-$3.75):**
- ⚠️ **Potentially too high** - $3.75 for one-time use is expensive
- ⚠️ **Tournament cost** - $19.63 to manually configure default rewards seems high
- ❌ **May discourage usage** - Players might save for "special occasions"

**Level 2-3 Items:**
- ⚠️ **Price jumps are significant** - $1.00 → $2.00 → $3.00 for Force Field
- ⚠️ **May feel expensive** - $3.00 for one game seems high

#### 3. **Tournament Reward Costs**

**Current Cost to Manually Configure Default Rewards:**
- Standard tier: **$19.63**
- Legendary tier: **$14.72**

**Concerns:**
- ⚠️ **High barrier to entry** - $19.63 is significant for tournament creation
- ⚠️ **Discourages custom rewards** - Most will use default (free)
- ⚠️ **May limit tournament variety** - Fewer custom tournaments

## Usage Psychology

### Why Players Don't Use Items

1. **Sunk Cost Fallacy**
   - "I paid $3.75 for this, I should save it for when I really need it"
   - **Solution:** Lower prices = less hesitation

2. **Hoarding Behavior**
   - "I have 50 items, I don't need to buy more"
   - **Solution:** Item merging encourages using items

3. **Fear of Wasting**
   - "What if I use it and die anyway?"
   - **Solution:** Lower prices = less fear

### Why Players Buy Items

1. **Impulse Purchases**
   - Under $2 is impulse territory
   - **Current:** Some items are too expensive

2. **Convenience**
   - "I need this now, don't want to wait for daily reward"
   - **Current:** Too many free items = less need

3. **Power Progression**
   - "I want Level 3 items, not Level 1"
   - **Current:** Price jumps may be too high
