# Revenue Projections & Financial Modeling

## Overview

This document provides financial projections and unit economics for the tournament platform SaaS, including revenue scenarios, cost analysis, and break-even calculations.

## Revenue Model Summary

### Revenue Streams

1. **Tournament Creation Fees**: $5.00 per tournament
2. **Platform Fees**: 5-10% of entry fees (explicit percentage, variable by tournament size)
3. **Platform Revenue (Remaining)**: Balance after creator and player rewards (also platform profit)
4. **Future: Pro Subscriptions**: $29/month (optional, future)

**Important:** All platform revenue (creation fees, platform fees, and remaining balance) goes to the same wallet/entity. The distinction is for accounting clarity only - they're all platform profit.

### Cost Structure

1. **Infrastructure**: Hosting, database, blockchain RPC
2. **Smart Contract Gas**: Tournament creation and operations
3. **Support**: Customer support, documentation
4. **Marketing**: Acquisition, partnerships

## Break-Even Analysis

### Platform Break-Even

**Monthly Fixed Costs: $400/month (average)**

**Revenue Needed:**
- At 70% margin: $400 / 0.70 = $571/month
- At 80% margin: $400 / 0.80 = $500/month

**Tournaments Needed:**
- Average revenue per tournament: $8.75 (medium tournament)
- At 70% margin: $571 / $8.75 = **65 tournaments/month**
- At 80% margin: $500 / $8.75 = **57 tournaments/month**

**Break-Even: ~60 tournaments/month**

---

### Creator Break-Even

**Creation Fee: $5.00**

**Break-Even:**
- 10 entries @ $1.00 = $10.00 entry fees
- Creator reward: $5.00 (50% of $10.00)
- **Net: $0.00** (breaks even)

**Profitability:**
- 20 entries: $7.00 creator reward = $2.00 profit
- 50 entries: $13.00 creator reward = $8.00 profit
- 100 entries: $23.00 creator reward = $18.00 profit

## Key Metrics

### Revenue Metrics
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) - from creation fees
- Platform Fee Revenue - explicit percentage of entry fees (5-10%)
- Platform Revenue (Remaining) - balance after distributions
- Total Platform Revenue - creation fees + platform fees + remaining (all same wallet)
- Average Revenue Per Tournament (ARPT)
- Average Revenue Per Creator (ARPC)

### Cost Metrics
- Cost Per Tournament (CPT)
- Infrastructure Cost Per Tournament
- Support Cost Per Tournament

### Profitability Metrics
- Gross Margin (70-85%)
- Net Margin (after all costs)
- Break-Even Point (~60 tournaments/month)
