### Narrative Tone:
- Mix of MewTwo (mystical, powerful entity) with Market themes (trading, volatility, bearish forces)
- Substantial but not extensive lore
- Each element should have both narrative context and gameplay utility

### UI Structure:
- **Tabs**: Horizontal navigation at top
- **Accordions**: Within each tab, collapsible sections
- **Images**: Display alongside narrative content
- **Responsive**: Works on both desktop and mobile

## Content Guidelines

### For Enemies/Bosses:
- Start with narrative (market symbolism + MewTwo style)
- Include gameplay stats (HP, speed, fire rate, points)
- Explain what they represent in the market theme
- Describe their behavior/patterns

### For Items:
- Narrative context (what it represents in market terms)
- Gameplay details (what it does, how to use)
- Pricing information
- Level differences (if applicable)

### For Systems:
- Narrative introduction (why it exists)
- How it works (mechanics)
- Benefits/strategies
- Integration with other systems
